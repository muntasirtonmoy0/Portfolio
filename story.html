Origin

I was born in a middle class family in Bangladesh. I stayed for almost 9 years in Rajbari, Bangladesh and completed my play, nursery and KG class education there. Later because of the transfer of my father's occupation as he is an service holder we moved to Noapara, Jashore, Bangladesh. There I studied from class 1-3. And again because of my father's occupation we moved to Chuadanga, Bangladesh. And there I studied from class 4-10.

The Spark (2021 — Self-taught)

In the time of covid-19, during lockdowns, I had pretty much time to spend here and there. Then I saw others building things online and something clicked. I wanted to understand it. All of it. No matter how hard. That's just who I am — if something pulls my interest, I chase it without looking back. Some of my online friend also fueled my thoughts by giving me instructions. Python was first. Then bots. Then everything else.

The Reality (Projects · Failures · Dust)

Most of my projects have failed. I gave them my time, ideas, and everything I had, only to watch them quietly fall apart. There were times I wanted to stop—to step away and let everything fade into silence. But somewhere inside, I kept finding a reason to continue. Failure may have changed my path, but it never ended my journey.

Never Stopped (Still Here Somehow)

Yet, somehow, I remain. I keep turning ideas into something real, even when the results don't go as planned. Perhaps I’m driven by a hope I can’t quite explain—the feeling that there’s still something worth creating ahead of me. So I continue, without needing recognition, building at my own pace and following a path only I can see.

THE LIFE — WHO I AM

Honestly? My life hasn't always been peaceful.

There are dreams—things I want more than I can put into words. Sometimes they feel impossibly far away, buried beneath difficulties, setbacks, and moments when everything seems to go against me.

There were times when I questioned whether I was capable of reaching them. But even then, something inside me kept whispering:

“Don’t stop. Don’t give up. Something good is being built for the future.”

So I keep going. I remind myself that great things take time, patience, and dedication. Maybe I haven't reached where I want to be yet—but I believe I can.

Every day, I keep building, learning, and moving forward.

Gaming — Things That Keep Me Going

Action · Open World · Survival · RPG

Gaming was more than just a way to pass time for me—it was a place where I found peace, laughter, and friendships I still remember. Some of the people I met encouraged my work, appreciated what I created, and guided me whenever I needed it.

As life moved forward, I eventually had to leave gaming behind and focus on other things. But I still carry those moments with me—the late nights, the conversations, the friendships, and the memories that made that chapter special.

I may have left the game, but I never left the memories.
